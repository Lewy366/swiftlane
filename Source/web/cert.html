Encrypt the connection to your PC

Once this is set up, everything between this phone and Swiftlane is encrypted, even at home. You only do this once.

First, check it's really your PC

Swiftlane on your PC shows this code under Connect phone. It must be exactly the same:

{{FINGERPRINT}}

iPhone / iPad

  1. Tap the button below and allow the download.
  2. Open Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install.
  3. Then Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → turn on Swiftlane.
  4. Come back here and tap Open encrypted Swiftlane.
Download the certificate

Android

  1. Tap the button below to download the certificate.
  2. Open Settings → search for Install certificate → CA certificate → choose Swiftlane.cer.
  3. Come back here and tap Open encrypted Swiftlane.
Download the certificate

Is this safe?

Yes. This certificate only works for addresses inside your home network and Tailscale. It can never vouch for websites like your bank, so it can't be misused to read your other traffic. You can remove it any time in Settings.
